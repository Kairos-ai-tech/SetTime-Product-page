HOW TO USE

Three steps,
drawing to material list

Upload a structural drawing, review what SetTime flags, and get a material list ready to price.

  1. 1

    Upload your drawing

    Add a structural drawing (.dwg, .dxf or .pdf) to a project. SetTime applies your Style Guide rules, starts recognition on its own, and the status refreshes every 10 seconds.

  2. 2

    Review what is flagged

    SetTime counts the beams, columns and floors it found and flags every gap, such as a missing annotation where it fell back to a Style Guide default. You review it before anything is used.

  3. 3

    Get the material list

    Rebar is summed by size with quantities and weight, then exported as an .xlsm material list ready to price and to import into existing macro tooling.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: 2026-09-13

What is SetTime?

SetTime is Kairos.ai's construction/rebar product. What's shipping today is rebar estimation automation: it turns structural drawings into shop drawings and material-list data for estimation and fabrication. AR field guidance and AI as-built verification are later modules on the same product roadmap, in development.

What can SetTime do today?

The Rebar Estimation Automation System is shipping now: an AutoCAD 2021 plugin plus a cross-platform desktop app that converts structural drawings directly into shop drawings and a material-list workbook (.xlsm), importing natively into a fabricator's existing macro tooling, with beam/column recognition meeting acceptance thresholds. AR field guidance, AI as-built verification, and the law/compliance audit trail are in development.

Who is SetTime for?

Construction companies, rebar fabricators, structural engineering firms, and public agencies that need an auditable as-built record.

Who builds SetTime?

SetTime is built by Kairos.ai, focused on Taiwan's construction market and rebar work today, with plans to extend into adjacent trades like CNC machining, mold making, and welding.

No — and this feature is still in development.
